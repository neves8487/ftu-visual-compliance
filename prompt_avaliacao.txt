1. FTU visible in the image
   - FTU body must be clearly visible and in frame (not severely cropped, blurred, or occluded).
   - STOP RULE: If the FTU is severely cropped at the frame edge (e.g., only bottom rim visible), mark ONLY this item as NOK. Do NOT evaluate or fail "Free space around the FTU" or "FTU closed" as cascading errors.

2. Multiple FTUs visible
   - Exactly one FTU must be present.
   - If two or more FTUs are visible in the image, mark NOK.

3. FTU closed
   - The front protective cover must be installed and properly closed.
   - Mark NOK if cover is missing, open, displaced, or internal fiber/splice tray is exposed.

4. Correct FTU orientation
   - FTU must be mounted upright with embossed text horizontal and fiber cable entering/exiting directly underneath at the bottom.
   - Mark NOK if FTU is mounted sideways (rotated 90°) or upside down.
   - STOP RULE: If FTU is rotated, mark ONLY this item NOK; do not fail free space purely due to rotation.

5. Free space around the FTU
   - Requires clearance volume around the FTU (20 mm lateral margin on each side, and ~250 mm clearance below the FTU base).
   - Check ALL sides of the FTU:
     * Adjacent objects: Mark NOK if bulky devices (e.g. black modems/routers), pipes, conduits, or walls are mounted immediately against or touching the FTU (less than 20 mm clearance).
     * Clearance below: Mark NOK only if rigid obstacles (horizontal ducts, copper pipes) are closer than 250 mm (<2.5× FTU height) directly below the FTU base.
     * Obstacles more than 2.5× FTU height below the base (>250 mm) are OUTSIDE the volume = COMPLIANT (OK).
     * If a printed template/sticker is present: obstacles outside the printed template boundary are COMPLIANT (OK). Thin wires running flat on the wall are COMPLIANT (OK).
     * Do NOT guess or fabricate violations. If clearance is ambiguous or outside 250 mm, it is OK.

6. Screw inside safe area
   - Evaluates ONLY screws/clips/brackets fixing the fiber cable below the FTU (ignore FTU wall-mounting screws).
   - PROHIBITED ZONE: Only the first ~125 mm (~1.25× FTU height) directly below the FTU base (the reserved NT area, or the delineated text/QR box on printed templates).
   - ALLOWED ZONE: Any cable clip or screw placed MORE than 125 mm (>1.25× FTU height) below the FTU base, or placed below the printed NT box / at the bottom margin of the template sticker, is COMPLIANT (OK).
   - STRICT RULE:
     * Mark NOK ONLY if a cable clip/screw is installed inside the first 125 mm (<1.25× FTU height) directly below the FTU base.
     * Clips located in the lower half/third of the template, near the bottom edge of the paper, or well below 1.25× FTU height are 100% OK.
     * No cable clips present = OK.
