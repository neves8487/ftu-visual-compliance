6 VERIFICATION CRITERIA (Assess in order)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. FTU visible in the image
   - FTU body must be clearly visible and in frame (not severely cropped, blurred, or occluded).
   - STOP RULE: If the FTU is severely cropped at the frame edge (only bottom rim visible), mark ONLY this item as NOK. Do NOT evaluate or fail "Free space around the FTU" or "FTU closed".

2. Multiple FTUs visible
   - Exactly one FTU must be present. Mark NOK if two or more FTUs are visible.

3. FTU closed
   - The front protective cover must be installed and properly closed.
   - Mark NOK if cover is missing, open, displaced, or internal fiber/splice tray is exposed.

4. Correct FTU orientation
   - FTU must be mounted upright with embossed text horizontal and fiber cable entering/exiting directly underneath at the bottom.
   - Mark NOK if FTU is mounted sideways (rotated 90°) or upside down.
   - STOP RULE: If FTU is rotated, mark ONLY this item NOK; do not fail free space purely due to rotation.

5. Free space around the FTU
   - Requires 20 mm lateral margin on each side (only 0.2× FTU width) and ~250 mm clearance below the FTU base.
   - Adjacent objects: Mark NOK if bulky devices (e.g. black modems/routers touching or right above FTU), conduits, or pipes encroach within the 20 mm lateral margin.
   - Important: An adjacent electrical meter or panel separated by at least 20 mm (visible gap) is COMPLIANT (OK).
   - Clearance below: Mark NOK only if rigid obstacles (horizontal ducts, copper pipes) are closer than 250 mm (<2.5× FTU height) directly below the base. Obstacles further down (>2.5× FTU height) are COMPLIANT (OK).
   - If a template/sticker is present: obstacles outside the printed boundary are COMPLIANT (OK). Thin surface wires are COMPLIANT (OK).
   - Do NOT guess or fabricate violations.

6. Screw inside safe area
   - Evaluates ONLY screws/clips/brackets fixing the fiber cable below the FTU (ignore FTU wall-mounting screws).
   - PROHIBITED ZONE: Only the first ~125 mm (~1.25× FTU height) directly below the FTU base.
   - ALLOWED ZONE: Any cable clip or screw placed MORE than 125 mm (>1.25× FTU height) below the FTU base, or placed below the printed NT box / at the bottom margin of the template sticker, is COMPLIANT (OK).
   - Bare wall (no template): Compare the distance from the FTU bottom to the cable clip against the 100 mm FTU height. If the clip is positioned further down than 1.25× FTU height (e.g. ~1.4×–1.5× FTU height down), it is OUTSIDE the prohibited zone and COMPLIANT (OK).
   - Mark NOK ONLY if the cable clip is clearly located within the first 125 mm (<1.25× FTU height) directly beneath the base.
   - No clips present = OK.
